Privacy

Short version: ClipNest does not collect anything about you, and neither does this website.

The app

  • Your recordings, clips and exports stay in the folders you choose on your own drive.
  • There is no account, no analytics and no telemetry.
  • On first launch the app downloads its recording engine (OBS Studio) from OBS’s official releases on GitHub. GitHub handles that download under its own privacy policy.
  • Settings live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ClipNest. Uninstalling keeps them, along with your clips.

This website

  • It is a static page on GitHub Pages, with no cookies, trackers or analytics of its own.
  • Fonts and scripts are served from this site, not from third parties.
  • GitHub may log visits to keep the service running, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Questions go to the issue tracker on GitHub.
